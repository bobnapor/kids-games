# Kids' Games

Six offline games for the phone home screen.

- **Rainbow Doodle**: finger drawing with a color-changing pen, sparkles, bubbles, stamps and a picture gallery.
- **Trace Champ**: trace shapes, numbers, letters and pictures; the closer you stay to the line, the higher the score.
- **Brick Builder**: build 3D brick models step by step (vehicles, space, buildings, animals and more), or make anything in Free Build.
- **Number Pop**: endless kindergarten math (counting, adding, taking away, more or less, missing numbers, make 10) with pictures, stars and stickers.
- **Holiday House**: build Christmas, Halloween and Valentine's decorations out of bricks, then put each one where it belongs in three houses (outside plus 2–3 rooms each).
- **Sound Safari**: speech sound practice (K and hard C, G, F, S, S-blends like "st", SH, plus your own words). Animated mouth guides, a mirror, "silly or right?" listening games, and say-it games that play his voice back. A press-and-hold grown-ups area lets you record words in your own voice. Microphone and camera stay on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere.

## Install on a phone

Open the site, tap a game, then:

- **iPhone (Safari):** Share button at the bottom → Add to Home Screen → Add
- **iPhone (Chrome):** Share button in the address bar → Add to Home Screen → Add (iOS 16.4 or later; if the option is missing, update Chrome or use Safari)
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → Add to home screen / Install app

Each game installs as its own app and works without internet after the first visit. Once added, a game runs on its own, whichever browser was used to add it.
