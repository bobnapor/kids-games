Put a game on your home screen

Open the game first, then add it. Each game gets its own icon and works without internet after the first visit.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap a game.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap a game.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Tap Add to home screen (or Install app), then Install.
